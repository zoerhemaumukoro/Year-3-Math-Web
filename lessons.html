Lesson ideas

Weekly maths lessons

Number patterns

  • Count in 2s, 3s, 4s, 5s and 8s.
  • Spot patterns in sequences and tables.
  • Use place value to explain each jump.

Quick check 1

What comes next: 4, 8, 12, 16, ?

Quick check 2

Which number is missing in this pattern: 5, 10, 15, __, 25?

Quick check 3

Count on in 3s from 9. What is the next number after 18?

Addition and subtraction

  • Use number bonds to 10 and 20.
  • Add and subtract tens and ones mentally.
  • Check answers by using the inverse.

Quick check 1

Which answer is correct for 38 + 17?

Quick check 2

What is 42 - 15?

Quick check 3

What is 19 + 26?

Times tables

  • Learn 3, 4 and 8 times tables.
  • Use arrays to show multiplication.
  • Match facts to related division facts.

Quick check 1

What is 7 × 8?

Quick check 2

What is 4 × 6?

Quick check 3

What is 8 × 3?

Fractions

  • Split shapes into equal parts.
  • Find fractions such as 1/2, 1/4 and 3/4.
  • Compare simple fractions with the same denominator.

Quick check 1

Which fraction is equal to 1/2?

Quick check 2

What is 3/4 of 8?

Quick check 3

Which is larger: 1/4 or 1/2?

Time and measure

  • Read analogue and digital clocks.
  • Measure length, mass and capacity.
  • Find the perimeter of simple shapes.

Quick check 1

How many centimetres are in 1 metre?

Quick check 2

What time is quarter past 4?

Quick check 3

A rectangle is 6 cm long and 3 cm wide. What is its perimeter?

Geometry and data

  • Identify right angles and symmetry.
  • Name 2D shapes and 3D objects.
  • Read information from bar charts and tables.

Quick check 1

How many right angles does a square have?

Quick check 2

Which shape has 3 sides?

Quick check 3

A bar chart shows 6 red, 4 blue and 2 green. Which colour is least popular?
