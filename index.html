Study tips

How to become a maths superstar

1. Learn number facts

Practise times tables, number bonds and mental maths every day.

2. Draw it

Use arrays, bar models and labelled diagrams to help solve problems.

3. Check your work

Estimate first, then review the answer to see if it makes sense.
